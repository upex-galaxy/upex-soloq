## Prioritization Criteria

ROI formula used: `(Frequency x Impact x Stability) / (Effort x Dependencies)`

Decision policy used:

- Candidate: ROI >= 1.5 and clear regression protection
- Manual: ROI 0.5-1.49 or low automation value but relevant for regression
- Deferred: one-time validation, low recurrence, or low product risk

## SQ-54 - Payment Method Capture

### Candidate tests

1. Validate configured payment methods are shown first and selected method is persisted in `payments.payment_method`.
2. Validate dropdown options include canonical enum values (`bank_transfer`, `paypal`, `mercado_pago`, `cash`, `other`) and save path remains functional when method is empty.

### Manual-only tests

1. Visual review of dropdown ordering and localized labels in modal (UI presentation only).

### Deferred

1. Analytics/reporting over payment method usage (out of scope of the story).

## SQ-56 - Payment Notes

### Candidate tests

1. Validate notes are persisted and rendered in payment details, including multiline and special characters.
2. Validate 500-char boundary (500 accepted, 501 rejected) with stable validation message.

### Manual-only tests

1. Visual behavior of remaining-character counter and textarea resizing.

### Deferred

1. Rich-text formatting and attachment behavior (explicitly out of scope).

## SQ-57 - Payment Date

### Candidate tests

1. Validate default payment date is today and persists correctly after submission.
2. Validate future-date rejection and required-date validation paths.
3. Validate pre-issue-date warning flow allows confirmation and still persists selected date.

### Manual-only tests

1. Calendar navigation UX and keyboard accessibility checks.

### Deferred

1. Locale customization and timezone personalization flows (out of scope).

## SQ-58 - Revert Payment

### Candidate tests

1. Validate revert flow soft-deletes payment, updates invoice status (`sent` vs `overdue` by due date), and clears paid indicators.
2. Validate revert creates `invoice_events` audit trail and updates dashboard totals (pending increases, paid decreases).
3. Validate cancel action in confirmation dialog keeps data unchanged.

### Manual-only tests

1. Visual warning content and confirmation dialog copy review.

### Deferred

1. Batch revert or undo behavior (explicitly out of scope).

## Output for Stage 5 Readiness

- Primary automation candidates are the persistence and state-transition flows listed above.
- Manual regression set is limited to visual/UX-only checks.
- Deferred cases are out-of-scope behaviors and do not enter regression now.
